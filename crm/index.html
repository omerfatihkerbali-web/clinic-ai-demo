The CRM: a real build

This is a CRM I built for a beauty academy (brand and data anonymised as "Glow Beauty Academy"). Instagram and WhatsApp messages land in one inbox, the AI assistant answers and qualifies leads, and every lead gets a card with its ad source, stage, next step and full history. The full system also has payments, reminders, calendar, contracts, staff and a client portal. The clinic version would use the same base, adapted to treatments, consultations and packages.
